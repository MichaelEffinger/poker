Bosses

**The Obvious Cheater** – A local legend who cannot be caught cheating. If a player tries to call out the cheater, they lose all the respect they've ever garnered.  
**The Bigstack Bully** – Starts with way more chips than you, putting pressure on others from the start.  
**The Millionaire Buy-In** – Gets to buy into the tournament two more times after busting, keeping them in the game longer.  
**The Drinker** – Starts the game with the best AI besides the dealer but gets worse the more drunk they get.  
**The Pot Shorter** – Intentionally shorts the pot the entire game, forcing others to adapt.  
**The Fortune Teller** – Gets to look into her crystal ball to see one of your cards, creating a new layer of strategy.  
**The Politician** – Does not have to be the little or big blind, providing more flexibility in play.  
**The Dealer** – The best player in the game who doesn’t cheat, making them a formidable force at the table.  
**The Muscular Bully** – Steals chips from you occasionally when they get pissed, adding a chaotic element to the game.  
**The Glutton** – A fat glutton who keeps staining the cards, adding a messy and chaotic dynamic to the game.  
**The Bride and Groom** – They trade with each other to make each other’s hands better, so you have to watch their coordination.  
**The Fog of War General** – Hides some part of the game, like the river or the flop, adding a sense of mystery.  
**The Extra River Card** – Gets a special golden river card that only they can use, creating an extra strategic edge.  
**The Statistician** – Uses their computer to check odds and plays based on that, turning the game into a data-driven challenge.  
**The LLM** – A large language model that needs a loser to actually make the moves for them, adding a humorous dynamic to the game. Similar to deep blue  
**Taxman –** Gets a percentage of the pot each time someone wins. Until the game starts winding down  
The Overseer: Places bets on who is gonna win the hand
